Frequently Asked Questions

What PowerPoint formats are supported? ▼

We support both .PPT (older PowerPoint format) and .PPTX (modern PowerPoint format) files. If you have presentations in other formats, you may need to convert them to one of these formats first.

Will my animations be preserved? ▼

Animations are converted to static images showing the final state of each animation. For complex animations with multiple steps, we recommend using the "Include speaker notes" option to document the animation sequence.

Can I convert password-protected PowerPoint files? ▼

Currently, we don't support password-protected PowerPoint files. Please remove the password protection before uploading your presentation for conversion.

How are speaker notes handled in the conversion? ▼

When you select the "Include speaker notes" option, each slide will be followed by a page containing its associated speaker notes. This creates a comprehensive document perfect for sharing with colleagues or as reference material.

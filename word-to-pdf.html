Frequently Asked Questions

What Word formats are supported? ▼

We support both .DOC (older Word format) and .DOCX (modern Word format) files. If you have documents in other formats, you may need to convert them to one of these formats first.

Will my formatting be preserved? ▼

Yes, our converter maintains fonts, colors, images, tables, and layout elements from your original Word document. Complex formatting like columns, headers, and footers are also preserved.

Is there a file size limit? ▼

The maximum file size for conversion is 50MB. For larger documents, consider splitting them into multiple files or compressing images before conversion.

How long are my files stored? ▼

Your files are automatically deleted from our servers within one hour of processing. We do not store or access your documents after conversion.

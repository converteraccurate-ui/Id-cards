Frequently Asked Questions

Will removing pages affect the quality of my PDF? ▼

No, the quality of your remaining pages will be preserved exactly as in the original document. We don't recompress or alter the content of pages you choose to keep.

Can I remove multiple pages at once? ▼

Yes, you can select multiple individual pages, use the range selector for consecutive pages, or use the bulk selection buttons to select all pages, even pages, or odd pages at once.

What happens if I remove all pages? ▼

If you select all pages for removal, the tool will create an empty PDF file. We recommend keeping at least one page to have a usable document.

Can I undo page removal? ▼

You can always go back and reselect pages before processing. Once the PDF is processed and downloaded, you would need to re-upload the original file to make different selections.

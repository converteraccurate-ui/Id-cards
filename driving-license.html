Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Digital Driving License?
A Digital Driving License is an electronic version of your physical driving license that can be downloaded and stored on your mobile device. It contains all the details of your original license and is legally valid for identification purposes.
Is Digital Driving License valid for identification?
Yes, the Digital Driving License is a valid proof of identity and driving authorization. It is legally recognized across India and can be used for various verification purposes, subject to acceptance by the requesting entity.
What if I can't find my driving license details online?
If you're unable to find your driving license details online, it could be due to incorrect details entered, database synchronization issues, or your license not being digitized yet. In such cases, contact your Regional Transport Office (RTO) for assistance.
Can I use the digital DL during traffic checks?
Yes, the digital driving license is legally valid and accepted by traffic police during checks. However, it's recommended to carry your physical license as a backup, especially in areas with poor network connectivity.

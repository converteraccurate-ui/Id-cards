Frequently Asked Questions

What is error correction in QR codes? ▼

Error correction allows QR codes to be scanned even if they are partially damaged or obscured. Higher error correction levels include more redundant data, making the QR code more resilient but also increasing its complexity and size.

Can I use custom colors for my QR code? ▼

Yes, you can customize both the QR code color and background color. However, ensure there's sufficient contrast between the two colors for reliable scanning. Dark on light combinations work best.

What's the maximum data a QR code can hold? ▼

Standard QR codes can hold up to 3KB of data. The exact capacity depends on the QR code version and error correction level. For most practical uses like URLs or contact information, this is more than sufficient.

Do QR codes expire? ▼

QR codes themselves don't expire. However, if your QR code points to online content (like a URL), that content might change or become unavailable over time. The QR code image will remain scannable indefinitely.
